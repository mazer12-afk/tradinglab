Day-trading research · 12 crypto perpetuals on Binance · 1-minute bars · developed Aug 2023 – Oct 2025 · tested 29 Oct 2025 – 29 Oct 2026

Day trading: one candidate survived its test year

Every idea was developed on data up to 29 Oct 2025 and judged after costs. Each rule taken to the test year was written down and committed to the repository before the following year was simulated once. Simulated fills use 1-minute bars: limit orders count only when the price trades through them, stops are assumed to fill before targets, and fees are Binance's 2 bp for limit orders and 5 bp plus spread for market orders.

Verdict

One candidate made money in the test year; it is not proven yet

Round 2 added Binance's 5-minute futures data (open interest, trader positioning, taker flow) and a model that learns which extreme moves to trade. Its best rule buys only when the model forecasts a strong 4-hour rise. Frozen before the test, it made +11.2 % in a year when the market fell 38 %, and all nine nearby settings made money too.

Two things keep it a candidate. It missed the development bar: after 37 attempts its deflated Sharpe was 0.41, where 0.95 was required. And one year of 135 trades is about one standard error from zero. The deciding evidence has to come from days that do not exist yet: a forward paper test.

What to do with this

  • Do not trade real money on it yet. One good year is not proof; a forward test on new days is the next step.
  • Know what it is. It buys only, about once every three days, and holds four hours. The model mostly times the whole market after sharp moves; it does not pick coins (against the market it loses).
  • Judge it on the forward test, frozen as committed (config/daytrade2_candidate.json), for at least three months before drawing a conclusion.

Round 2 · The candidate

A model that times the market after sharp moves

Every 5-minute move beyond 3 standard deviations in one of the 12 coins is an event. A LightGBM model, retrained at the start of each month on past events only, forecasts the coin's next 4 hours from 23 features: the move's size and coin-specific part, open-interest change over 5 minutes, an hour and a day, taker buy/sell flow, top-trader and crowd long/short ratios, the last funding rate, the market's return over an hour, a day and three days, volatility, and the time. When the forecast exceeds +60 bp the rule buys at market and sells 4 hours later; 20 % of equity a trade, at most 1× equity open.

Round 1 · Fading extreme moves

Fading both directions worked for a year, then failed

Take the other side of a 5-minute move beyond k standard deviations with a limit order, hold up to 4 hours. On the design period all 81 variants of the extended grid made money (best: Sharpe 2.2, +72 % a year). On the second half of development all 81 lost (the same variant: Sharpe −0.75, −30 %). Splitting the trades by direction showed why.

Round 1 · Buy the crash

The frozen rule and its one test

After a 5-minute close more than 8 standard deviations below the previous one, post a limit buy 1 standard deviation lower, valid 10 minutes; sell at market 4 hours after the fill. No stop, no target; 20 % of equity per trade, open positions capped at 1× equity. Chosen as the best of 64 long-only variants on the design period, then committed before the test.

Method

How the simulator fills a trade

  • Decisions use bars up to the close of the decision minute; orders fill from the next minute on.
  • Market orders fill at the next minute's open plus the coin's typical live half-spread and 1 bp of impact, paying 5 bp. Limit orders fill at their price only if a later minute trades at least 1 bp through it, paying 2 bp; unfilled, they expire.
  • A stop fills before a take-profit when both are reached in the same minute, at the stop or the worse open. After a limit entry, exits are checked from the next minute.
  • Funding is paid or received at every settlement held through. One trade per coin at a time; total open positions capped.
  • Seven tests in tests/test_daytrade.py check these rules on hand-built bars, including that nothing before or at the decision minute can change a fill.
  • Protocol: ideas and parameters on Aug 2023 – Aug 2024; checked on Sep 2024 – Oct 2025; one frozen rule run once on 29 Oct 2025 – 29 Oct 2026.
