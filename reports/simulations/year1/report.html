Simulated experiment · year1 · 29 Oct 2025 → 29 Oct 2026 · daily book, 40 accounts

A year with only the past winners: one of ten made money

Strategies that lost money in development were cut, the winners were improved and new ones added, all on data up to 29 Oct 2025. The frozen roster then ran once through the simulator over the following year: the live engine and trading rules, with costs, on archived prices. Each strategy ran four times: without a vault, and with a vault that banks all profit above $100,000 once equity reaches +1 %, +2 % or +4 %. Every account started at $100,000.

Verdict

Past profit did not carry into the next year

It was a bear year: the 30-coin basket fell 38 %, and 58 % at its low in July. Only the long/short trend strategy made money (+2.2 %). The long-or-flat trend strategies, which had Sharpe ratios near 1 from 2021 to 2025, lost 8–19 %, and the blend lost 11 %. They lost far less than holding the coins, but they lost.

LightGBM made no money either. It was cut before the test because no version earned its costs in development. Run over the test year anyway, the 5-minute models lost 23 % and 57 %, and the new daily model lost 21 %. Of the seven daily strategies that were cut, four would have made money this year; of the seven kept, improved or new ones, one did.

What to do with this

  • Do not trade real money on any of these strategies. None shows an edge that survives a change of market.
  • Stop choosing strategies by recent profit alone. Replayed every January since 2020, the same keep-the-winners rule picked the better group in only 4 of 7 years. Judge a strategy by how it behaves across bull, bear and choppy years.
  • Retire the 5-minute book at taker fees. Costs decide it, not the forecasts: LightGBM 4h broke even before costs in the test year and lost 23 % after them. Revisit only with cheaper execution, such as maker orders, which the simulator would first need to model.
  • If anything continues, pair long/short trend with long-or-flat trend, which made money in different years, and evaluate the pair over several years of rolling tests instead of one window.
1 · Market

Down 58 %, up 74 %, down again

The equal-weight basket of the 30 daily coins peaked on 11 November 2025 and fell to 1 July 2026, 58 % below the start. It then rallied 74 % to 6 October, mostly in September, and gave back 16 % by the end. Over the year it fell 37.9 %, and Bitcoin 27.9 %.

3 · Choosing the roster

The development winners lost; four of the cut strategies made money

Every decision used data before 29 Oct 2025, from walk-forward out-of-sample backtests net of costs. Kept: out-of-sample Sharpe of at least 0.5 over 2021–2025 and over the last two years, and a profitable last year. Improved or new: accepted only if better than the parent strategy in 2021–23 and again in 2023–25. The blend was chosen by the research selection on 2021–2025. The test-year column comes from the simulator for the strategies that ran, and from the same backtester for the rest.

Test-year return of every daily strategy, kept or cut
On average the kept group (−11.0 %) and the cut group (−10.4 %) did the same. Pruning changed which strategies won, not how much the group made.
Why the overlap with earlier results is not used

The live fortnight (Oct 2026) and the Jul–Sep 2026 simulation fall inside this test year. Pruning on their losses and then testing on the same months would make the survivors look good by construction, so they played no part in the choice.

The 5-minute models: costs decide it

LightGBM 4h's forecasts broke even before costs in the test year, then paid 23 % of capital in fees and spread on 361 times its capital traded. LightGBM 1h lost 27 % even before costs. The forecasts carry a little information (earlier research measured a rank correlation of about 0.07 between the next-hour forecast and the outcome), worth roughly 4 bp per trade against a 12–16 bp round trip.

Its +7.7 % in the live fortnight was a two-week result. Over Jul–Sep 2026 in the simulator the same model lost 18 %, and over 2023–25 it lost about 38 % a year.

The daily model: wrong direction, then costs

The new daily model averaged 44 % net long in a falling year: its forecasts alone lost 13 %, and fees and spread on 101 times its capital took the total to −21 %. In development it made 6.7 % a year, but only 0.20 Sharpe in 2021–23, well below the long-or-flat trend rules it was competing with (about 1.0).

5 · Does pruning work?

Keeping the winners picked the better group in 4 of 7 years

The same rule (keep a strategy if its two-year Sharpe is at least 0.5 and its last year was profitable) applied on 1 January of each year and at the start of this test, using only earlier data, then measured over the next 12 months. Daily rule-based strategies, research backtests.

The rule chose the better group in 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2024. It failed in 2023, when it had dropped the long-or-flat trend strategies after the 2022 crash just before the cut group gained 60 % on average, and in 2025 and this test year, when it kept them just before they lost. A strategy's past profit mostly reflects the market it ran in.

6 · The vault

Banking profit cost $3.8k net this year

13 of the 30 vault twins reached their threshold at least once; the other 17 finished level with their control. Five of the 13 ended ahead. The long/short trend twin at 1 % banked 20 times and finished $3,286 ahead of its control. Across the lab's three runs the vault helped where gains kept reversing (the falling fortnight: +$11.8k) and hurt in a trend (Jul–Sep 2026: −$80.7k).

7 · Method

How the year was simulated

  • The simulator runs the live daily engine on archived Binance data with a simulated clock: hourly marks, a decision each day at 03:00:30 UTC, fills at the archived price plus each coin's typical live spread, a 5 bp impact charge and a 5 bp fee, and settled funding. On the live run of 15–29 Oct 2026 it reproduced every trade of every daily account without a vault, with a median gap of $45 per $100k.
  • For the strategies in this test, the simulator and the research backtester agree within about 2 points over the year.
  • Development used walk-forward out-of-sample backtests on data before 29 Oct 2025: daily strategies from 2021 (5 bp fee + 5 bp spread per side), 5-minute strategies on spot bars from mid-2023 with LightGBM retrained on the last 365 days (5 bp fee + 1–3 bp spread per side, plus funding).
  • Rejected without a test-year run: market-trend filters (they cut 2021–23 Sharpe to 0.24), slower trend lookbacks (worse in 2021–23), and a filter on crowded funding, which helped only with same-day funding data that the live engine does not receive.
  • Reproduce: tradinglab --config config/year1.yaml simulate --books daily --start 2025-10-29 --end 2026-10-29 --out runs/year1 (35 minutes on 4 CPU cores).
